Iokharic - font required for the Vs'shtak (Draconic) edition
============================================================

The font files themselves are deliberately NOT part of this repository.
Their licence forbids redistribution, so they have to be fetched once by
hand before the `vss` edition can be built.

What is needed
--------------

Place these four files next to this note, in
`language_files/vss/fonts_vss/`:

    Iokharic-Regular.otf
    Iokharic-Bold.otf
    Iokharic-Italic.otf
    Iokharic-BoldItalic.otf

Where to get them
-----------------

Iokharic, by Neale Davidson (Pixel Sagas), version 1.00 of 21 August 2014.

    https://www.pixelsagas.com/            (the author's own site)
    https://www.dafont.com/iokharic.font   (mirror)

The archive ships the files as `Iokharic.otf`, `Iokharic Bold.otf`,
`Iokharic Italic.otf` and `Iokharic Bold Italic.otf`. Rename them to the
hyphenated names listed above; `language_files/vss/fonts_vss.tex` looks
for exactly those, and the spaces in the original names are a portability
risk on Overleaf and Linux.

Pixel Sagas shareware under the EULA bundled with the archive as
`Font License.txt`. The two clauses that matter here:

  - "Pixel Saga's Shareware Fonts are free to use for personal,
     non-commercial purposes. [...] However, you cannot offer the font
     for commercial sale, or offer for direct download."
  - "Pixel Sagas freeware fonts may, however, be embedded for web,
     publication, or general software use."

Embedding the glyphs in the rendered PDF is therefore covered. Shipping
the `.otf` files in this repository would be "offering for direct
download" and is not, which is why they are listed in `.gitignore`.

downstream users commercial rights that this font licence does not.
Anyone redistributing or commercially reusing the Vs'shtak edition needs
a commercial licence from Pixel Sagas (jaynz@pixelsagas.com).

The other language editions are unaffected: `deu`, `eng`, `lat` and `qya`
build without this font.
